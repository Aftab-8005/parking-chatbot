# Airport Parking

## General Information

Airport Parking provides secure parking spaces for travelers using the International Airport.

The parking facility supports short-term and long-term (multi-day) parking reservations, suited for travelers who will be away for several days.

The facility is monitored by CCTV and has on-site staff around the clock.

## Location

Parking Name: Airport Parking

Address: 25 Terminal Road, International Airport

The parking facility is located directly adjacent to the airport terminal, with a free shuttle service to the departures hall.

## Parking Spaces and Features

The parking facility offers standard parking spaces as well as a covered section for added weather protection.

Airport Parking is open 24 hours a day to accommodate early and late flights.

Parking availability changes depending on existing reservations. Current availability, working hours, and prices are dynamic values and should be looked up live rather than assumed from this document.
