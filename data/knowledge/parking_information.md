# Parking Chatbot — General Information

## Supported Facilities

Parking Chatbot supports reservations and information lookup for the following parking facilities:

- City Center Parking — 100 Main Street, City Center
- Airport Parking — 25 Terminal Road, International Airport
- Railway Station Parking — 8 Platform Avenue, Central Railway Station
- Mall Parking — 500 Commerce Boulevard, Grand Mall

Each facility has its own working hours, pricing, and availability, which are dynamic values looked up live rather than assumed from this document. See each facility's own knowledge document for its specific location and features.

## Booking Process

A user can request a parking reservation through the chatbot at any of the supported facilities.

The chatbot collects:

- Which parking facility (City Center Parking, Airport Parking, Railway Station Parking, or Mall Parking)
- First name
- Surname
- Car number
- Reservation start time
- Reservation end time

If the user does not mention a facility, the chatbot defaults to City Center Parking.

The reservation request is then submitted for administrator approval.

A reservation is not considered confirmed until the administrator approves it.

## Reservation Rules

Reservations must be made for a valid period within the chosen facility's operating hours.

The reservation start time must be earlier than the reservation end time.

A parking reservation cannot be confirmed when the requested parking space is unavailable.

The user should provide accurate reservation information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which parking facilities do you support?

City Center Parking, Airport Parking, Railway Station Parking, and Mall Parking.

### Can I reserve a parking space through the chatbot?

Yes. The chatbot can collect the required reservation information, including which facility you want, and submit the request for approval.

### Is a reservation immediately confirmed?

No. The reservation requires administrator approval before it is final.

### What information is required for a reservation?

The chatbot collects the parking facility, the user's first name, surname, car number, reservation start time, and reservation end time.

### Can I ask about parking prices, availability, or working hours?

Yes, but those are dynamic values that change over time and differ per facility, so the chatbot looks them up live instead of relying on this static document.
