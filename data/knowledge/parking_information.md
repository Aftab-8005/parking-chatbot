# City Center Parking

## General Information

City Center Parking provides secure parking spaces for cars in the city center.

The parking facility supports short-term and daily parking reservations.

The parking facility has standard parking spaces suitable for regular passenger cars.

The facility is monitored by CCTV and has on-site staff during operating hours.

## Location

Parking Name: City Center Parking

Address: 100 Main Street, City Center

The parking facility is located near the main commercial and business district, within walking distance of the central train station.

## Parking Spaces

The parking facility offers standard parking spaces intended for passenger vehicles.

Parking availability changes depending on existing reservations. Current availability, working hours, and prices are dynamic values and should be looked up live rather than assumed from this document.

## Booking Process

A user can request a parking reservation through the chatbot.

The chatbot collects:

- First name
- Surname
- Car number
- Reservation start time
- Reservation end time

The reservation request is then submitted for administrator approval.

A reservation is not considered confirmed until the administrator approves it.

## Reservation Rules

Reservations must be made for a valid period within the parking operating hours.

The reservation start time must be earlier than the reservation end time.

A parking reservation cannot be confirmed when the requested parking space is unavailable.

The user should provide accurate reservation information.
