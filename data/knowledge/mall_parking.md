# Mall Parking

## General Information

Mall Parking provides parking spaces for shoppers and visitors at the Grand Mall.

The parking facility supports short-term parking for shopping trips as well as daily reservations for events.

The facility is monitored by CCTV and has on-site staff during mall opening hours.

## Location

Parking Name: Mall Parking

Address: 500 Commerce Boulevard, Grand Mall

The parking facility is located in the multi-level parking structure attached to the Grand Mall.

## Parking Spaces and Features

The parking facility offers standard parking spaces as well as reserved spaces near the mall entrance for families and accessible parking.

Mall Parking's operating hours match the mall's opening hours; current hours, availability, and prices are dynamic values and should be looked up live rather than assumed from this document.
